# COPYRIGHT.md — Copyright & DMCA (RHYTHM)

*Last updated: 6 November 2026 · Policy version `2026-11-06`.*

Mirrors the in-app **Copyright & DMCA** page (`copyrightView()` in `app.js`).

We respect copyright and respond to valid notices of infringement. RHYTHM is a
prototype that stores content locally, but reports are reviewed by an
administrator and production will use the same process with a designated
grievance officer.

## 1. How to send a takedown notice

Email **[PLACEHOLDER: PRIVACY / GRIEVANCE EMAIL]** with the subject
“Copyright takedown” and include all of the following:

- Your full name, address, telephone number and email address.
- Identification of the copyrighted work you say is infringed (title, author,
  registration if any, and a link or copy).
- The exact URL, post title or username of the allegedly infringing material,
  with enough detail for us to find it.
- A statement that you have a good-faith belief the use is not authorised by
  the copyright owner, its agent or the law.
- A statement that the information in your notice is accurate and, under
  penalty of perjury, that you are the owner or authorised to act for the owner.
- Your physical or electronic signature.

## 2. What happens next

We aim to acknowledge valid notices within 3 business days. We may remove or
restrict the material, notify the uploader, and ask for a counter-notice. We may
also remove repeat infringers and suspend or terminate their accounts. We keep
a record of notices and actions for legal compliance.

## 3. Counter-notice

If your content was removed by mistake or you have authorisation, email
**[PLACEHOLDER: PRIVACY / GRIEVANCE EMAIL]** with the subject “Copyright
counter-notice”. Include your name, address, phone, email, identification of
the removed material and where it appeared, a statement under penalty of
perjury that you have a good-faith belief the removal was a mistake or
misidentification, your consent to the jurisdiction of the appropriate court,
and your signature. We may restore the material unless the original complainant
starts legal action within the period required by law.

## 4. Misrepresentation

Knowingly making a false notice or counter-notice can lead to liability for
damages, including costs and legal fees. Do not use this process to suppress
legitimate criticism or competition.

## 5. Repeat infringers

Accounts that repeatedly upload infringing material may be suspended or
permanently terminated.

## 8. In-app reporting

Signed-in users can report a post or a user from the post card / profile. The
report reasons include copyright/plagiarism and child safety. Admins review
reports in the admin dashboard and can remove posts, suspend users, or dismiss
the report.
