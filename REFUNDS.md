# REFUNDS.md — RHYTHM Refund & Cancellation Policy

*Last updated: 6 November 2026 · Policy version `2026-11-06`.*

Mirrors the in-app **Refund & Cancellation Policy** (`refundView()` in
`app.js`). Items in `[BRACKETS]` are owner placeholders.

> **Current status: no real payments.** RHYTHM is a prototype. Auctions and
> fees are simulated, no card or bank details are collected, and RHYTHM does
> not charge you. This page explains the production policy that will apply when
> payments go live.

## 1. Prototype (today)

Nothing on RHYTHM costs money today. There is no checkout, no subscription and
no charge, so no refund or cancellation is needed. Any “₹” amounts are
illustrative demo data.

## 2. Production: auctions and licences

When real payments are enabled, the following will apply. A winning bid forms
a contract for the rights described in the listing. The total payable — winning
bid, platform fee and applicable taxes — will be shown **before** you confirm.
Bids are binding once accepted. If a seller cannot deliver the described rights
or files, the buyer may request a refund within
**[PLACEHOLDER: REFUND WINDOW, e.g. 7 days]** of the scheduled delivery date.

## 3. Cancellations

You may cancel a bid before the auction closes if the interface allows it.
After close, cancellation is at the seller's and RHYTHM's discretion and may
affect your account. Sellers may cancel a listing before the first bid; after a
bid, cancellation requires a valid reason and may lead to suspension. Statutory
cancellation rights are not affected.

## 4. Fees

Platform fees are disclosed before you bid and are generally non-refundable
once the transaction completes, except where required by law or where RHYTHM
cancels the transaction. If a transaction is refunded, the platform fee will be
refunded pro rata unless the refund results from the buyer's breach.

## 5. Failed or duplicate payments

If a payment fails, is reversed, or is charged twice, contact
**[PLACEHOLDER: PRIVACY / GRIEVANCE EMAIL]** with the transaction reference. We
will investigate with the payment gateway and refund verified duplicate or
failed charges to the original payment method.

## 6. Subscriptions

RHYTHM has no subscription today. If one is introduced, cancelling will be as
easy as subscribing, and the cancellation terms will be shown before purchase.

## 7. Chargebacks and disputes

Please contact us before raising a chargeback so we can resolve it. Fraudulent
chargebacks may lead to suspension. Nothing here limits your right to dispute
a charge with your bank or card issuer.

## 8. How to request a refund

Email **[PLACEHOLDER: PRIVACY / GRIEVANCE EMAIL]** or use the in-app Contact
page with your username, transaction reference, amount and reason. We aim to
acknowledge within 3 business days and decide within 30 days (or the period
required by law). Approved refunds go to the original payment method; bank
processing times vary.

## 9. Statutory rights

Nothing in this policy excludes or limits rights you have under mandatory
consumer protection law in your country, including India's Consumer Protection
Act 2019.
